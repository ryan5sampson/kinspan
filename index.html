Bring your family

Drop your family file here
A .kinspan file you saved before, or a .ged export from FamilySearch, Ancestry, MyHeritage or RootsMagic.

Your file never leaves this device. It is read here, in your browser. There is no account, no server, and nothing is uploaded.

Your file is your save. Play, write people's stories, fix what the records got wrong, add anyone who is missing — then save it back out. That one file is what you keep, and what you send to your family.

Who is this family centred on?

Kinspan describes everyone in relation to one person — your grandmother, her second cousin. Pick whoever the tree is built around, usually you.
