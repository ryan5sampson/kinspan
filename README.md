# Kinspan

A hand of your own family. Every Kin is a real person from your own family tree:
put them back on the timeline where they lived — husbands and wives, parents and
children, whole lines of them — and earn Legacy for what you reconnect.

**Play it: https://ryan5sampson.github.io/kinspan/**

## Bring your own family

This site ships with **nobody in it**. You bring a file:

- a **`.ged`** export from FamilySearch, Ancestry, MyHeritage or RootsMagic, or
- a **`.kinspan`** save file you made here before.

Your file is read **in your browser, on your device**. It is never uploaded.
There is no account, no server and no database — this is a folder of static
files. Nothing about your family goes anywhere.

## Your file is your save

After the first import, the `.kinspan` file is the thing you keep. Play, write
people's stories, fix what the records got wrong, add anyone who is missing —
then save it back out. That one file is yours, and it is what you send to your
family so they can pick up where you left off.

A GEDCOM can't hold a story, a Gift, or a person you added, which is why the
save file exists.
